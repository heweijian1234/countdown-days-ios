Countdown Widget - Days — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2026

The short version

Countdown Widget - Days does not collect any data. Everything stays on your device. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking of any kind.

How the app works

  • Your events (names, dates, icons) are stored only on your device
  • The home screen widgets read those events through iOS App Group storage — locally, on your phone
  • The app works fully offline; it never requests your location, contacts, photos, or any other permission

What we never do

  • We never upload, transmit, or sell anything
  • We never track you, profile you, or share data with third parties
  • There is nothing to share: the app has no network access at all

Data removal

Deleting the app removes all of your events immediately and completely. There is no server copy, because nothing was ever sent anywhere.
